## Withdrawing before the deadline

Sample policy for this learning project only: A student who wants to leave a course before the published withdrawal deadline should submit the course withdrawal form through the student portal. The student's academic adviser can explain how withdrawal may affect the study plan.

## Withdrawing after the deadline

Sample policy for this learning project only: Requests made after the published deadline require a written explanation and review by the academic office. The office sends its decision to the student. This sample does not describe real university rules.
