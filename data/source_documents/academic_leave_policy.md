## Requesting academic leave

Sample policy for this learning project only: Students who need a break from study may send an academic leave request to Student Services before the semester begins. The request should include the student's name, student ID, reason, and requested leave dates. Student Services reviews the request and sends its decision by email.

## Returning to study

Sample policy for this learning project only: Before returning, a student should contact Student Services and confirm the planned return date. The office checks the student's record and explains any steps needed to register for classes again.
